About Herndon Roofing

Herndon Roofing has covered the Baltimore metro area for 35+ years from its shop at 3150 Wilkens Ave, with a second location in Linthicum Heights. The shop installs and repairs TPO, EPDM and hot asphalt flat roofs, shingles and standing seam metal, and runs its own metal fabrication, welding and soldering in house, the kind of specialty work most roofing companies have to send out.
